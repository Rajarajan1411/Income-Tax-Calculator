Calculation scope & sources

For resident individuals with salary, bank interest, one let-out property and other ordinary slab-rate income. Excludes capital gains, lottery/crypto income, business income, agricultural-income integration, foreign income, arrears relief and carried-forward losses. Do not enter these in other income.

Rental income uses rent less municipal taxes paid, a 30% deduction on net annual value and eligible loan interest. Old-regime house-property loss set-off is capped at ₹2 lakh; new-regime losses are not set off against other heads. Unused losses are not tracked. Savings interest relief is limited to ₹10,000 below age 60; eligible bank-interest relief is limited to ₹50,000 for seniors under the old regime.

Enter legally eligible exemptions and deductions, not gross spending. Employer NPS must already be included in salary and entered separately for each regime after applying the applicable salary-percentage limit. Health insurance amounts require eligibility checks for self/family and parents. Donation input is the eligible deduction after percentage and qualifying-income limits. Affordable-home/EV interest requires a qualifying loan; do not double-count housing interest. No automatic eligibility determination is made.

Taxable income and final tax are rounded to the nearest ₹10. Includes rebate, rebate marginal relief, surcharge marginal relief and 4% cess. FY 2025–26 and FY 2026–27 use the same slabs. Descriptive deduction labels avoid mixing section numbering between the 1961 and 2025 Acts.

Covered by the Act: last drawn monthly salary × 15 ÷ 26 × years of service. Not covered: last drawn monthly salary × 15 ÷ 30 × years of service.
